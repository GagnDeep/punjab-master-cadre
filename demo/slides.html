Elizabethan Literature

The Golden Age of English Poetry & Drama

Key Characteristics

  • Unprecedented cultural flowering during Elizabeth I's reign (1558–1603).
  • Strong spirit of nationalism and religious tolerance.
  • Revival of classical learning (Renaissance influence).
  • Development of blank verse and the English sonnet.

Major Playwrights

  • William Shakespeare
  • Christopher Marlowe
  • Ben Jonson
  • Thomas Kyd
  • John Webster
  • Francis Beaumont
  • John Fletcher
  • Thomas Middleton
