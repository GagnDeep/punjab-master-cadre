The Elizabethan Age & Christopher Marlowe

Age of Shakespeare • University Wits

The Elizabethan Era

  • 1558–1603: Reign of Queen Elizabeth I.
  • Golden Age of English literature.
  • Heavy influence of the Renaissance and Humanism.
  • Rise of professional theatres (e.g., The Globe).

Christopher Marlowe

  • Most significant predecessor to Shakespeare.
  • Popularized 'Blank Verse' (unrhymed iambic pentameter).
  • Known for the 'Marlowe's mighty line' (coined by Ben Jonson).
  • Themes of overreaching ambition and hubris.

Marlowe's Major Works

  • Doctor Faustus: Selling soul for knowledge.
  • Tamburlaine the Great: Lust for power.
  • The Jew of Malta: Lust for wealth.
  • Edward II: Weakness in leadership.
