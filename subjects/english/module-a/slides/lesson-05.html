John Milton

Paradise Lost • Puritanism

John Milton & The Puritan Age

  • Deeply religious, classical scholar, and republican.
  • Went completely blind in 1652 ('On His Blindness').
  • Wrote during the turbulent English Civil War period.

Paradise Lost (1667)

  • The greatest epic poem in English (written in blank verse).
  • Stated Purpose: 'To justify the ways of God to men.'
  • 1st Edition (1667): 10 Books.
  • 2nd Edition (1674): 12 Books (matching Virgil's Aeneid).

Satan's Mantra

Better to reign in Hell, than serve in Heaven.

The defining quote of Satan, demonstrating his pride and rebellion in Book 1 of Paradise Lost.

Other Major Works

Poetry / Drama

  • Paradise Regained (Sequel, 4 books)
  • Samson Agonistes (Tragic closet drama)
  • Lycidas (Pastoral Elegy)

Prose

  • Areopagitica
  • (A passionate defense of freedom of speech and the press)
