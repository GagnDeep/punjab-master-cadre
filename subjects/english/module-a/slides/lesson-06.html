Ben Jonson & The Comedy of Humours

Cavalier Poets • Jacobean Drama

Ben Jonson

  • Contemporary and rival to Shakespeare.
  • England's first unofficial Poet Laureate.
  • Advocated for strict adherence to classical dramatic rules.
  • Followers were known as the 'Tribe of Ben'.

Comedy of Humours

  • Genre popularized by Jonson.
  • Based on medieval medical theory of 4 bodily fluids.
  • Characters have an imbalance causing one dominant, obsessive personality trait.
  • Goal is to satirize and 'cure' these extreme behaviors.

Jonson's Major Plays

Title

  • Every Man in His Humour
  • Volpone (The Fox)
  • The Alchemist

Premise

  • Established the Humours genre.
  • A bitter satire on extreme greed.
  • A farce about con artists and gullibility.

The Cavalier Poets

  • Royalist poets supporting King Charles I during the Civil War.
  • Poetry was light, elegant, and secular.
  • Dominant theme: 'Carpe Diem' (Seize the Day).
  • Major figures: Robert Herrick, Richard Lovelace, John Suckling.

Carpe Diem Quote

Gather ye rosebuds while ye may

The famous opening line by Cavalier poet Robert Herrick, urging youth to enjoy life before time runs out.
