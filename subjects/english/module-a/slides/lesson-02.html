Shakespeare's Sonnets

  • Published in 1609, total of 154 sonnets.
  • Structure: 3 quatrains + 1 couplet (abab cdcd efef gg).
  • Sonnets 1-126: Addressed to the 'Fair Youth'.
  • Sonnets 127-152: Addressed to the 'Dark Lady'.
