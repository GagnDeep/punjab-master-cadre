Edmund Spenser & The Faerie Queene

Allegory • Spenserian Stanza

Edmund Spenser

  • Known as 'The poet's poet' (coined by Charles Lamb).
  • Used deliberate archaic language to emulate Chaucer.
  • Major works include The Shepheardes Calender and The Faerie Queene.

The Faerie Queene: Allegory

The Knight

  • Redcrosse Knight (Book I)
  • Sir Guyon (Book II)
  • Britomart (Book III)

The Virtue

  • Holiness
  • Temperance
  • Chastity

Political Allegory

  • Gloriana (The Faerie Queene) represents Queen Elizabeth I.
  • Arthur represents the ideal of magnificence.
  • Duessa represents Mary, Queen of Scots (and falsehood).

The Spenserian Stanza

  • Invented specifically for The Faerie Queene.
  • 9 lines total.
  • Lines 1-8: Iambic Pentameter.
  • Line 9: Iambic Hexameter (known as an Alexandrine).
  • Rhyme Scheme: ababbcbcc.
