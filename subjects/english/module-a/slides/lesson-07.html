Restoration Age & John Dryden

Heroic Couplet • Satire

The Restoration Era (1660)

  • Monarchy restored (King Charles II).
  • Theatres reopened after being closed by Puritans in 1642.
  • Women acted on the English stage for the first time.
  • Literature shifted to worldly, cynical, and satirical tones.

John Dryden

  • The dominant figure ('Age of Dryden').
  • England's first official Poet Laureate (1668).
  • Perfected and popularized the Heroic Couplet.
  • Master of political and mock-heroic satire.

Dryden's Major Works

Satires

  • Mac Flecknoe (Mocking poet Thomas Shadwell)
  • Absalom and Achitophel (Biblical allegory for politics)

Drama & Prose

  • All for Love (Adaptation of Antony & Cleopatra)
  • An Essay of Dramatic Poesy (Criticism)

Restoration Comedy

  • Known as the 'Comedy of Manners'.
  • Satirized the affectations and hypocrisies of the upper class.
  • Famous for witty dialogue (repartee) and complex plots.
  • Pinnacle work: William Congreve's 'The Way of the World'.

Aphra Behn

First

Recognized as the first English woman to earn a living as a professional writer. Famous for her novel 'Oroonoko'.
