Rise of the Novel: Addison & Fielding

Periodical Essays • Picaresque Novel

The Periodical Essay

  • Emerged in the early 18th century alongside coffee-house culture.
  • Aimed to bring philosophy and learning to the middle class.
  • Richard Steele founded 'The Tatler'.
  • Joseph Addison and Steele co-wrote 'The Spectator'.

The Rise of the Novel

  • A shift away from fantastical romances toward realism.
  • Focused on ordinary people in everyday situations.
  • Driven by a growing, increasingly literate middle class.
  • The 'Four Wheels' established the genre's foundations.

Defoe & Richardson

Daniel Defoe

  • Robinson Crusoe (1719)
  • Pioneer of extreme realism.
  • Focus on survival and individualism.

Samuel Richardson

  • Pamela, Clarissa
  • Invented the Epistolary Novel (letters).
  • Focus on intense psychology and morals.

Fielding & Smollett

Henry Fielding

  • Tom Jones, Shamela
  • Wrote 'comic epics in prose'.
  • Picaresque tradition (wandering rogue).

Tobias Smollett

  • Roderick Random
  • Rough, action-packed picaresque.
  • Eccentric characters (often sailors).

Epistolary Novel

Letters

The defining format of Samuel Richardson's works, allowing deep psychological insight into the characters.
