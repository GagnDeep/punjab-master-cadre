Preparation Guidance

The Punjab Master Cadre English exam consists of 150 questions covering English literature, Indian English writers, literary terms, grammar and linguistics, and pedagogy. Our course is structured into five modules to help you master these topics systematically.

🧠 Study Phase Strategy

Start with Foundation (Modules C, D, E) to grasp the mechanics of language and literary terminology. Then move to the Core (Modules A, B) to tackle the vast ocean of literature. Finish with intensive Revision using mock tests.
