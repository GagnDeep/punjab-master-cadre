Sports as a Socializing Agent

  • Sports acts as a "mini-society."
  • Teaches norms, values, and rules.
  • Promotes cooperation and fair play.

Task Cohesion

  • Working together to achieve a specific goal.
  • E.g., executing a complex play to win the match.

Social Cohesion

  • Liking each other.
  • Enjoying team company outside of the sport.

Leadership: Autocratic

  • "My way or the highway."
  • Leader makes ALL decisions.
  • Good for emergencies or very inexperienced teams.

Leadership: Democratic

  • "Let's decide together."
  • Leader asks for team input.
  • Builds trust and intrinsic motivation.
  • Best for mature teams.

Quick Recap

  • Socialization = Learning society's rules.
  • Task Cohesion = Focus on winning/goal.
  • Autocratic = Strict, no input.
  • Democratic = Shared decision making.
