Growth vs. Development

  • Growth: Quantitative, measurable (Height, Weight)
  • Development: Qualitative, functional (Skills, Cognition)

Characteristics of Growth

  • Cell multiplication
  • Stops at maturity
  • Physical changes only

Characteristics of Development

  • Continuous lifelong process
  • Includes cognitive and social changes
  • Proceeds from general to specific responses

Stages: Infancy (0-2)

  • Rapid physical growth
  • Basic motor skill acquisition
  • Sensory exploration

Stages: Childhood (2-12)

  • Early (2-6): Fine motor refinement
  • Later (6-12): Steady growth, highly active
  • Socialization with peers

Stages: Adolescence (12-18)

  • Puberty and rapid physical changes
  • Emotional volatility
  • Identity formation

Principles of Development

  • Cephalocaudal: Head to toe
  • Proximodistal: Center to extremities
  • Individual differences exist

ERB Exam Focus

  • Distinguish qualitative vs quantitative
  • Identify characteristics of Adolescence
  • Understand developmental direction principles

Recap

  • Growth stops, Development continues
  • Adolescence is a critical transition
  • Directional principles guide motor learning
