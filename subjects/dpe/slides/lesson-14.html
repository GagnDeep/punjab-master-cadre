Components of Fitness

Physical Fitness

  • Ability to meet life's demands without undue fatigue
  • Crucial for athletes and general health
  • Comprises 5 main motor components

1. Strength

  • Maximal force exerted against resistance
  • Maximum Strength: Heavy lifting
  • Explosive Strength: Power/Jumping

2. Endurance

  • Ability to resist fatigue
  • Aerobic: With oxygen (Marathon)
  • Anaerobic: Without oxygen (Sprint)

3. Speed

  • Performing movements in shortest time
  • Reaction time + Movement speed
  • Highly dependent on genetics (fast-twitch fibers)

4. Flexibility

  • Range of motion around a joint
  • Active Flexibility: Moving independently
  • Passive Flexibility: With external help

5. Coordinative Abilities

  • Agility, balance, rhythm
  • Controlled by the Central Nervous System
  • Crucial for complex sports skills

Measuring Components

  • Strength: 1RM tests
  • Endurance: 12-min run/walk
  • Flexibility: Sit and Reach test

ERB Exam Focus

  • Differentiate Aerobic vs Anaerobic
  • Identify Flexibility definition (joints)
  • Know tests used for measurement

Recap

  • Strength, Endurance, Speed, Flexibility, Coordination
  • Aerobic = Long distance
  • Anaerobic = Sprints/Power
