Modern Psychology

  • Historically: Study of the "soul".
  • Currently: The scientific study of behavior and mental processes.
  • Sports Psych applies this to enhance performance.

Thorndike's 3 Laws of Learning

  1. Law of Readiness: Must be physically/mentally prepared to learn.
  2. Law of Exercise: Practice strengthens habits (Use it or lose it).
  3. Law of Effect: Rewards strengthen behavior; punishments weaken it.

Intrinsic Motivation

  • Comes from WITHIN.
  • Joy, mastery, personal satisfaction.
  • Highly durable.

Extrinsic Motivation

  • Comes from OUTSIDE.
  • Medals, money, fame, avoiding punishment.
  • Can fade if reward is removed.

Personality Types (Jung)

  • Introverts: Quiet, inwardly focused. Excel in closed, individual sports (Archery).
  • Extroverts: Outgoing, need stimulation. Excel in fast, team sports (Football).

Quick Recap

  • Psychology = Study of behavior.
  • Law of Effect = Behavior + Reward = Habit.
  • Intrinsic = Internal (Joy).
  • Extrinsic = External (Medal).
