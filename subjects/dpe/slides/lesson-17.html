What is First Aid?

  • Immediate care given to an injured person
  • Provided before professional medical help arrives
  • Aims to preserve life and prevent worsening

Primary Aims (The 3 Ps)

  • PRESERVE life (CPR, stop bleeding)
  • PREVENT condition from worsening
  • PROMOTE recovery

PRICES Protocol

  • Standard care for soft tissue injuries
  • Used in the first 48-72 hours
  • Minimizes swelling and bleeding

P - R - I

  • Protection: Prevent further damage
  • Rest: Stop activity immediately
  • Ice: Apply cold to constrict vessels (vasoconstriction)

C - E - S

  • Compression: Elastic bandage to physically limit swelling
  • Elevation: Raise above heart to drain fluid
  • Support: Splints or crutches

Why Ice and Elevate?

  • Ice causes vessels to shrink, stopping internal bleeding
  • Elevation uses gravity to pull fluid away from injury site
  • Prevents joint stiffness later

Basic Life Support (CPR)

  • Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation
  • Used when athlete is unresponsive and not breathing
  • Maintains oxygenated blood flow to brain

Emergency Action Plan

  • Every facility must have an EAP
  • Clear communication protocols
  • Access to AEDs (Defibrillators)

ERB Exam Focus

  • Know what EVERY letter in PRICES stands for
  • Understand WHY compression is used
  • Aim of First Aid: Preserve, Prevent, Promote

Recap

  • PRICES = Protect, Rest, Ice, Compress, Elevate, Support
  • First aid bridges the gap to paramedics
  • Ice and elevation manage swelling
