Communicable Diseases

Module 5 • Lesson 11

ਛੂਤ ਦੀਆਂ ਬਿਮਾਰੀਆਂ

What are Communicable Diseases?

  • Infectious diseases
  • Caused by pathogens
  • Transmitted between hosts

Types of Pathogens

  • Bacteria (e.g., TB, Typhoid)
  • Viruses (e.g., Measles, COVID-19)
  • Protozoa (e.g., Malaria)
  • Fungi (e.g., Ringworm)

Modes of Transmission

  • Direct Contact (Touch, Fluids)
  • Droplet (Sneezing, Coughing)
  • Vector-borne (Mosquitoes)
  • Vehicle-borne (Water, Food)

Bacterial Diseases

  • Tuberculosis: Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Lungs)
  • Typhoid: Salmonella typhi (Intestines)

Viral Diseases

  • Measles: Highly contagious respiratory virus
  • Polio: Affects nervous system
  • Rabies: Transmitted via animal bites

Vector-Borne Diseases

  • Malaria: Plasmodium protozoa via female Anopheles
  • Dengue: Virus via Aedes mosquito

Prevention Strategies

  • Vaccination/Immunization
  • Personal Hygiene & Sanitation
  • Vector Control (Nets, Spraying)

ERB Exam Focus

  • Match pathogen type to disease
  • Know vector for Malaria (Female Anopheles)
  • Identify modes of transmission

Recap

  • Pathogens: Bacteria, Virus, Protozoa
  • TB = Bacteria, Malaria = Protozoa
  • Prevention is key in PE settings
