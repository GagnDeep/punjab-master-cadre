The Skeleton

  • Adults have 206 bones.
  • Axial (80): Skull, spine, ribs (the core).
  • Appendicular (126): Limbs, shoulders, hips.

Key Bones

  • Femur: Thigh (Longest & strongest).
  • Tibia: Shinbone.
  • Humerus: Upper arm.
  • Scapula: Shoulder blade.

Joint Types

  • Immovable (Skull)
  • Slightly movable (Spine)
  • Freely movable (Synovial)

Synovial Joints

  • Hinge: Knee, Elbow
  • Ball & Socket: Shoulder, Hip
  • Pivot: Neck

Types of Muscles

  • Skeletal (Voluntary): Used in sports, attaches to bones.
  • Smooth (Involuntary): Internal organs.
  • Cardiac (Involuntary): Heart only, doesn't fatigue easily.

Muscle Contractions ⭐️

  • Isometric: "Same length". Pushing a wall. Force generated, NO movement.
  • Isotonic: "Same tension". Lifting a weight. Muscle shortens or lengthens.
  • Isokinetic: "Same speed". Special machines.

Quick Recap

  • Femur = Longest bone.
  • Ball & Socket = Highest range of motion.
  • Skeletal Muscle = Voluntary.
  • Isometric = Static (No movement).
  • Isotonic = Dynamic (Movement).
