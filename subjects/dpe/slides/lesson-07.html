Digestive & Excretory Systems

Module 3 • Lesson 7

The Digestion Path

  1. Mouth: Chewing + Saliva.
  2. Esophagus: Food pipe.
  3. Stomach: Acid breakdown.
  4. Small Intestine: Main digestion area.
  5. Large Intestine: Water absorption.

The Small Intestine ⭐️

  • Where maximum digestion and nutrient absorption takes place.
  • It is actually much longer (~20ft) than the Large Intestine.

Liver

  • Largest internal organ.
  • Produces Bile.
  • Stores energy (glycogen).

Gallbladder

  • Small pouch under liver.
  • Stores the Bile until needed.

The Excretory System

  • Function: Filter blood and remove waste.
  • Kidneys: The primary organs. Filter blood to create urine.
  • Skin: Also excretes waste via sweat.

The Nephron

  • The microscopic filtering unit of the Kidney.
  • Millions of nephrons work to clean your blood every day.
  • Analogy: Neuron is to Brain as Nephron is to Kidney.

Quick Recap

  • Small Intestine = Max Absorption.
  • Large Intestine = Water Absorption.
  • Liver makes Bile; Gallbladder stores it.
  • Kidney filters blood.
  • Nephron = Kidney's basic unit.
