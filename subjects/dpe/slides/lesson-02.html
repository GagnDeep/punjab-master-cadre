Sparta

  • Goal: Purely military
  • Train fierce warriors
  • System: Agoge

Athens

  • Goal: Well-rounded citizens
  • Harmony of mind and body
  • Modern concept of PE

Ancient Greek Vocab

  • Palaestra: Wrestling school.
  • Paidotribe: PE Teacher.
  • Didascaleum: Music/intellectual school.
  • Gymnasiarch: Head of the gymnasium.

Don't mix up Palaestra (place) and Paidotribe (person)!

Philosophies (1)

  • Idealism (Plato): Mind over matter. Character building. Winning is secondary.
  • Naturalism (Rousseau): Follow nature. Child-centered. No rigid rules.

Philosophies (2)

  • Pragmatism (John Dewey): "Learning by doing". Experience is key. Problem-solving.
  • Realism: Focus on science, facts, anatomy, and biomechanics.

Quick Recap

  • Sparta = Warriors; Athens = Mind+Body.
  • Palaestra = Wrestling school.
  • Dewey = Pragmatism (Experience).
  • Rousseau = Naturalism (Nature).
