The Heart

  • 4 Chambers: 2 Atria (top), 2 Ventricles (bottom).
  • Left side pumps oxygenated blood to the body.
  • Right side pumps deoxygenated blood to the lungs.

Arteries

  • Carry blood AWAY.
  • Thick, elastic walls.
  • Usually oxygenated.

Veins

  • Bring blood BACK.
  • Have valves to stop backflow.
  • Usually deoxygenated.

The Big Exception 🚨

Pulmonary Artery: The ONLY artery with deoxygenated blood (goes to lungs).

Pulmonary Vein: The ONLY vein with oxygenated blood (comes from lungs).

Key Cardio Terms

  • Stroke Volume (SV): Blood pumped per beat.
  • Heart Rate (HR): Beats per minute.
  • Cardiac Output (CO): SV × HR (Total blood per minute).
  • BP Norm: 120/80 mmHg.

Respiratory System

  • Alveoli: Tiny air sacs in lungs. This is where gas exchange (O2 in, CO2 out) happens.
  • Vital Capacity: Max air exhaled after max inhalation. A great measure of fitness.
  • Second Wind: Relief from fatigue during steady-state exercise when oxygen supply meets demand.

Quick Recap

  • A for Artery = Away.
  • Capillaries = Gas exchange.
  • Alveoli = Lung sacs.
  • Cardiac Output = Heart Rate × Stroke Volume.
