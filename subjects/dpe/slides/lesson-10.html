WHO Definition of Health

  • Established in 1948
  • Not merely the absence of disease
  • A state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being

Physical Health

  • Optimal functioning of bodily systems
  • Nutrition, exercise, and rest
  • Resistance to disease

Mental & Emotional Health

  • Ability to process information
  • Coping with stress effectively
  • Expressing emotions appropriately

Social Health

  • Building healthy relationships
  • Effective communication
  • Adapting to social situations

The Wellness Continuum

  • Health is not static
  • Moves between premature death and optimal wellness
  • Influenced by daily choices

Factors Influencing Health

  • Genetics/Heredity
  • Environment (Physical & Social)
  • Lifestyle & Behavior Choices

Preventive Medicine

  • Primary: Preventing onset (Vaccines)
  • Secondary: Early diagnosis (Screening)
  • Tertiary: Rehabilitation

ERB Exam Focus

  • Memorize the exact WHO phrasing
  • Understand that health is multi-dimensional
  • Know the difference between health and wellness

Recap

  • Health = Complete well-being
  • Dimensions: Physical, Mental, Social
  • Lifestyle choices drive wellness
