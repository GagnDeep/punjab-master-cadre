Module 3 • Lesson 6

The Nervous System

  • CNS: Brain + Spinal Cord. (The Command Center)
  • PNS: Nerves connecting CNS to the body.
  • Neuron: The basic working unit of the brain/nerves.

Cerebrum

  • Largest part.
  • Thinking, memory.
  • Voluntary movement.

Cerebellum

  • "Little brain".
  • Balance & Coordination.
  • Crucial for athletic skill.

Endocrine System

  • Uses hormones (chemical messengers) instead of electrical signals.
  • Hormones are released directly into the blood.

The Master Gland

  • Pituitary Gland: Located in the brain.
  • Controls all other glands.
  • Produces Growth Hormone (GH).

Fight or Flight!

  • Adrenal Glands: Sitting on the kidneys.
  • Produce Adrenaline (Epinephrine).
  • Increases heart rate, gives sudden energy burst.

Quick Recap

  • Neuron = Nerve Cell.
  • Cerebellum = Balance.
  • Pituitary = Master Gland.
  • Thyroid = Metabolism.
  • Adrenaline = Emergency/Sports readiness.
