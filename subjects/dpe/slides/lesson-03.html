The Father of Modern PE in India

  • H.C. Buck
  • Founded YMCA College of Physical Education
  • Location: Madras (Chennai)
  • Year: 1920

Pre-Independence Milestones

  • 1920: YMCA Madras.
  • 1927: Indian Olympic Association (IOA) formed.
  • Sir Dorabji Tata: First IOA President.

Post-Independence Committees

  • 1948: Tara Chand Committee (First advisory committee).
  • 1953: Rajkumari Amrit Kaur Coaching Scheme (First systematic coaching).
  • 1954: All India Council of Sports (AICS).

LNIPE (1957)

  • Location: Gwalior
  • Focus: PE Degrees
  • 1st Principal: Dr. P.M. Joseph

NSNIS (1961)

  • Location: Patiala
  • Focus: Coaches Training
  • Asia's largest sports institute

Sports Authority of India (SAI)

  • Established: 1984
  • Purpose: Manage national teams, promote sports.
  • Merged with NIS Patiala in 1987.

Dates to Memorize

  • 1920 - YMCA (H.C. Buck)
  • 1927 - IOA (Dorabji Tata)
  • 1953 - Amrit Kaur Scheme
  • 1957 - LNIPE (P.M. Joseph)
  • 1961 - NIS Patiala
  • 1984 - SAI
