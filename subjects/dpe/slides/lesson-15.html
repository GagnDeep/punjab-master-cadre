Warm-up & Training Methods

Module 7 • Lesson 15

ਵਾਰਮ-ਅੱਪ ਅਤੇ ਸਿਖਲਾਈ ਦੇ ਤਰੀਕੇ

The Warm-up

  • Physiological and psychological preparation
  • Increases core temperature and blood flow
  • Reduces risk of soft tissue injury

The Cool-down

  • Gradually lowers heart rate
  • Prevents blood pooling in extremities
  • Helps clear lactic acid from muscles

Continuous Training

  • Long duration, no rest intervals
  • Heart rate remains steady
  • Develops aerobic endurance base

Interval Training

  • Alternating periods of work and active recovery
  • Specific work-to-rest ratios
  • Builds cardiovascular fitness quickly

Fartlek Training

  • Swedish for 'Speed Play'
  • Continuous running with varied intensities
  • Based on athlete's feel and terrain

Circuit Training

  • Performing exercises at multiple stations
  • Targets various muscle groups sequentially
  • Improves strength and endurance simultaneously

Principles of Training

  • Overload: Pushing beyond normal limits
  • Specificity: Training relevant energy systems
  • Reversibility: Use it or lose it

ERB Exam Focus

  • Know Fartlek means 'Speed play'
  • Identify Interval training structure
  • Understand purpose of Cool-down (lactic acid)

Recap

  • Warm-up prepares; Cool-down recovers
  • Intervals use work/rest ratios
  • Fartlek varies speed randomly
