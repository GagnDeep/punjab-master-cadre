Not Just "Playing Games"

  • PE is an integral part of the total education process.
  • Aim: Improvement of human performance and holistic development.
  • Mind, Body, and Social Self are all connected.

Physical Training (PT)

  • Focus: Body only
  • Goal: Fitness & Discipline
  • Scope: Narrow

Physical Education (PE)

  • Focus: Whole person
  • Goal: Holistic development
  • Scope: Broad (mental, social)
"Physical education is the sum of changes in the individual caused by experiences centering in motor activity."

— Rosalind Cassidy

Keyword trick: sum of changes / motor activity = Cassidy

"Physical education is that phase of education which deals with big muscle activities and their related responses."

— Voltmer and Esslinger

Keyword trick: big muscle = Voltmer

H. Clark's 3 Objectives

  1. Physical Fitness: Strength, endurance, agility.
  2. Social Efficiency: Teamwork, sportsmanship.
  3. Culture: Societal aspects of sports.

Quick Recap

  • PE aims for holistic development (body + mind).
  • Cassidy = "motor activity".
  • Voltmer = "big muscle".
  • H. Clark's 3 Objectives: Fitness, Social, Culture.
