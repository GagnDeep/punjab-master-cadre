What is a Balanced Diet?

  • Contains all essential nutrients in correct proportions
  • Maintains health and supports growth
  • Varies based on age, gender, activity

Macronutrients

  • Carbohydrates: Primary immediate energy
  • Proteins: Tissue growth and repair
  • Fats: Stored energy and hormone production

Micronutrients

  • Vitamins: Regulate metabolic processes
  • Minerals: Bone health (Calcium), oxygen transport (Iron)
  • Provide NO caloric energy

Hydration

  • Water regulates body temperature
  • Transports nutrients
  • Critical for athletic performance

Alcohol as an Intoxicant

  • Depressant on Central Nervous System
  • Impairs coordination and reaction time
  • Causes severe dehydration

Tobacco (Smoking)

  • Carbon Monoxide binds to hemoglobin
  • Reduces oxygen transport to muscles
  • Decreases lung capacity

Drugs in Sports

  • Anabolic steroids: Unfair advantage, health risks
  • Stimulants: Mask fatigue
  • Strictly prohibited (WADA)

ERB Exam Focus

  • Carbs = Immediate Energy
  • Protein = Muscle Repair
  • CO from smoking blocks oxygen transport

Recap

  • Macros provide energy; Micros regulate
  • Intoxicants actively destroy performance
  • Smoking kills aerobic capacity
