Common Sports Injuries

Module 8 • Lesson 16

ਆਮ ਖੇਡ ਸੱਟਾਂ

Soft Tissue Injuries

  • Most common in sports
  • Affect muscles, tendons, ligaments, and skin
  • Require immediate basic care

Sprain vs. Strain

  • SPRAIN: Injury to a LIGAMENT (Bone to Bone)
  • STRAIN: Injury to a MUSCLE or TENDON (Muscle to Bone)
  • Remember: sTrain = Tendon

Skin Injuries

  • Abrasion: Scraping away outer skin layer
  • Contusion: Bruise from direct blow (internal bleeding)
  • Laceration: Jagged, irregular cut

Bone Injuries (Fractures)

  • Break in bone continuity
  • Simple/Closed: Stays beneath skin
  • Compound/Open: Breaks through skin (infection risk)

Specific Fracture Types

  • Greenstick: Incomplete break (common in children)
  • Comminuted: Shattered into multiple pieces
  • Stress: Micro-fractures from overuse

Joint Injuries

  • Dislocation: Bone forced out of alignment
  • Subluxation: Partial dislocation that reduces on its own
  • Requires medical realignment

Causes of Injuries

  • Poor conditioning or warm-up
  • Faulty equipment or surfaces
  • Overtraining/Overuse

ERB Exam Focus

  • Absolutely know Sprain (Ligament) vs Strain (Tendon)
  • Compound fracture breaks the skin
  • Greenstick is common in kids

Recap

  • Sprains affect joints; Strains affect muscles
  • Contusions are bruises
  • Open fractures are severe medical emergencies
