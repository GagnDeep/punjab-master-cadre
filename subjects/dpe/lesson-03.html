Lesson 3: Institutions & Committees in India

ਪਾਠ 3: ਭਾਰਤ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੰਸਥਾਵਾਂ ਅਤੇ ਕਮੇਟੀਆਂ

Why this matters (ERB 2017, 2022)

Questions like "When was LNIPE established?" or "Who was the first president of the IOA?" are guaranteed fact-based questions. Memorizing these dates and names is essential.

ਸੰਸਥਾਵਾਂ ਦੀ ਸਥਾਪਨਾ ਦੇ ਸਾਲ (ਜਿਵੇਂ LNIPE, SAI) ਪੇਪਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਲਾਜ਼ਮੀ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਨ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Know the establishment years of key institutions: YMCA (Madras), LNIPE (Gwalior), and SAI.
  • Identify the first presidents of major Indian sports bodies (IOA).
  • Understand the purpose of various national committees and schemes (Tara Chand Committee, Rajkumari Amrit Kaur Coaching Scheme).

1. Pre-Independence Milestones

Before 1947, physical education was largely influenced by British systems and indigenous akharas.

  • 1920: YMCA College of Physical Education, Madras (Chennai). Founded by H.C. Buck (known as the father of modern physical education in India). This was the first dedicated PE college in Asia.
  • 1927: Indian Olympic Association (IOA) formed. Sir Dorabji Tata was the first President, and Dr. A.G. Noehren was the Secretary.
  • 1932: Christian College of Physical Education, Lucknow.
  • 1938: Training Institute of Physical Education, Kandivali (Mumbai).

2. Post-Independence Committees & Schemes

After independence, the government took active steps to formalize PE and sports.

  • 1948: Tara Chand Committee. Formed to suggest ways to develop PE. It recommended a Central Advisory Board of Physical Education.
  • 1950: Central Advisory Board of Physical Education and Recreation (CABPER). Formed on the recommendation of the Tara Chand Committee.
  • 1953: Rajkumari Amrit Kaur Coaching Scheme. Introduced by the then Health Minister to provide top-class coaching to Indian athletes. It was the first systemic coaching scheme in India.
  • 1954: All India Council of Sports (AICS). Established to advise the government on sports matters. First president: Gen. K.M. Cariappa.

3. Major National Institutions

  • 1957: Lakshmibai National College of Physical Education (LNCPE/LNIPE), Gwalior. Established on the centenary of the 1857 revolt. First Principal: Dr. P.M. Joseph. It offered the first 3-year degree course in PE in India.
  • 1961: Netaji Subhas National Institute of Sports (NSNIS), Patiala. Established to produce high-caliber coaches. (Often referred to simply as NIS Patiala).
  • 1984: Sports Authority of India (SAI). Created to promote sports broadly, manage national teams, and maintain stadiums built for the 1982 Asian Games. It absorbed NIS Patiala in 1987.

Worked Example

Q: Who is known as the father of modern physical education in India?

  1. Dr. P.M. Joseph
  2. Sir Dorabji Tata
  3. H.C. Buck
  4. Baron Pierre de Coubertin

Step 1: Identify the names. Dr. P.M. Joseph was the first principal of LNIPE. Sir Dorabji Tata was the first IOA president.

Step 2: Recall that H.C. Buck founded the YMCA College in Madras in 1920, pioneering modern PE.

Answer: C. H.C. Buck.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

Match the columns are common here. You must perfectly link the year to the institution (1920-YMCA, 1957-LNIPE, 1961-NSNIS, 1984-SAI).

Quick Recap

  • 1920: YMCA Madras (H.C. Buck)
  • 1927: IOA (Sir Dorabji Tata)
  • 1948: Tara Chand Committee
  • 1953: Rajkumari Amrit Kaur Coaching Scheme
  • 1957: LNIPE Gwalior (Dr. P.M. Joseph)
  • 1961: NSNIS Patiala (Coaches)
  • 1984: SAI (Sports Authority of India)
