Lesson 9: Social Aspects & Group Dynamics

ਪਾਠ 9: ਸਮਾਜਿਕ ਪਹਿਲੂ ਅਤੇ ਸਮੂਹ ਗਤੀਸ਼ੀਲਤਾ (Group Dynamics)

Why this matters (ERB 2019, 2021)

Leadership styles (Autocratic vs. Democratic) and the process of socialization through sports are very common pedagogy questions. The examiner wants to see if you understand how to manage a team.

ਲੀਡਰਸ਼ਿਪ ਦੀਆਂ ਕਿਸਮਾਂ ਅਤੇ ਖੇਡਾਂ ਰਾਹੀਂ ਸਮਾਜੀਕਰਨ ਦੇ ਸਵਾਲ ਆਮ ਪੁੱਛੇ ਜਾਂਦੇ ਹਨ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Understand the role of sports as a socializing agent.
  • Define "Group Cohesion" and its impact on team performance.
  • Identify the three main leadership styles: Autocratic, Democratic, and Laissez-Faire.

1. Sports and Socialization

Socialization is the process by which individuals learn the values, beliefs, and norms of a society. Sports act as a powerful "mini-society."

Through sports, athletes learn:

  • Cooperation: Working towards a common goal.
  • Rule following: Understanding that society has laws (rules of the game).
  • Fair play & Sportsmanship: Respecting opponents and officials.

2. Group Dynamics & Cohesion

A group is not just a collection of people; it's individuals interacting with a shared purpose. Group Cohesion is the glue that keeps a team together.

  • Task Cohesion: How well team members work together to achieve a specific goal (e.g., winning the championship).
  • Social Cohesion: How much team members like each other and enjoy each other's company.

Note: A team can have high task cohesion but low social cohesion and still win (they don't have to be best friends to execute a play), but having both is ideal.

3. Leadership Styles in Sports

How a coach or captain leads directly affects team dynamics.

  • Autocratic (Dictatorial): The leader makes all the decisions. "My way or the highway." Useful in emergencies or with very inexperienced teams needing strict direction.
  • Democratic (Participative): The leader involves the team in decision-making. Builds trust and intrinsic motivation. Best for mature, experienced teams.
  • Laissez-Faire (Delegative): "Let them do." The leader gives the team complete freedom to make decisions. Rarely effective in competitive sports, but can work for highly skilled, self-motivated professionals in a relaxed setting.

Worked Example

Q: A coach who asks the senior players for input on the weekend's game strategy is displaying which leadership style?

  1. Autocratic
  2. Democratic
  3. Laissez-Faire
  4. Dictatorial

Step 1: Identify the action: The coach is asking for input / sharing decision-making.

Step 2: This rules out Autocratic (where the coach decides alone).

Step 3: The coach is still involved, so it's not Laissez-Faire (total freedom).

Answer: B. Democratic.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

They will give you a scenario of a very strict coach and ask you to name the style (Autocratic). Or they will ask what the primary social benefit of sports is (learning fair play/socialization).

Quick Recap

  • Socialization = Learning society's rules through sports.
  • Task Cohesion = Working together to win.
  • Social Cohesion = Liking each other.
  • Autocratic = Coach decides everything.
  • Democratic = Team has a voice.
