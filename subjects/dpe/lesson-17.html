Lesson 17: Principles of First Aid

ਪਾਠ 17: ਮੁੱਢਲੀ ਸਹਾਇਤਾ ਦੇ ਸਿਧਾਂਤ

Why this matters

When an athlete goes down, the first 5 minutes are critical. Do you know the PRICES protocol? ERB tests the exact steps of initial injury management.

ਜਦੋਂ ਕੋਈ ਖਿਡਾਰੀ ਡਿੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ, ਤਾਂ ਪਹਿਲੇ 5 ਮਿੰਟ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹੱਤਵਪੂਰਨ ਹੁੰਦੇ ਹਨ। ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ PRICES ਪ੍ਰੋਟੋਕੋਲ ਜਾਣਦੇ ਹੋ?

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Understand the primary aims of First Aid.
  • Explain and apply the PRICES protocol for soft tissue injuries.
  • Understand the basic principles of CPR.

1. Aims of First Aid

First aid is the immediate assistance given to a sick or injured person until professional medical help arrives. The primary aims are to: 1) Preserve life, 2) Prevent the condition from worsening, and 3) Promote recovery.

2. The PRICES Protocol

For most soft tissue injuries (sprains, strains), the immediate treatment protocol is PRICES:

  • P - Protection: Protect from further injury (e.g., crutches).
  • R - Rest: Stop the activity to allow healing.
  • I - Ice: Apply cold to reduce bleeding and swelling (never directly on skin).
  • C - Compression: Wrap with an elastic bandage to control swelling.
  • E - Elevation: Raise the injured part above the heart level to reduce fluid accumulation.
  • S - Support: Provide mechanical support if needed.

Worked Example

Q: In the initial treatment of a sprained ankle using the PRICES method, what does the 'E' stand for?

  1. Exercise
  2. Energy
  3. Elevation
  4. Extension

Step 1: Recall the PRICES acronym: Protection, Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation, Support.

Step 2: Match 'E' to Elevation.

Answer: C (Elevation)

🧠 How ERB Asks It

Acronyms are heavily tested. Know what every letter in PRICES stands for. Sometimes they just ask about RICE or PRICE—the letters mean the same things.

Quick Recap

  • First aid: Preserve life, prevent worsening, promote recovery.
  • PRICES: Protect, Rest, Ice, Compress, Elevate, Support.
  • Ice reduces swelling; Elevation reduces blood pooling.
