2. Societal Influences

Indus Valley Civilization art is characterized by its utilitarian nature and use of varied materials. The emphasis was on function and realistic representation of humans and animals rather than monumental structures.

3. Patronage & Mediums

Patrons

  • Royal Courts
  • Religious Institutions

Materials

  • Locally sourced stone
  • Natural mineral pigments

Focus!

Spatial organization is highly intentional in Indus Valley Civilization Art.

4. Symbolism

Flora, fauna, and geometric patterns serve dual purposes: aesthetic enhancement and allegorical communication.

5. Regional Variations

Do not conflate sub-schools. Each region developed unique techniques that distinguish it from its neighbors.

Extended Analysis

The lack of deciphered script means we rely heavily on these visual artifacts to understand their religion, trade, and society. The focus on geometric patterns and animal motifs is prevalent.

Historical Timeline Focus

It is crucial to map these developments against contemporary movements globally to fully grasp their significance.

Did you know?

Many artifacts from this period were originally painted in vibrant colors, which have faded over time.
