The Cell Cycle: Focus on S Phase

  • $G_1$ $\rightarrow$ Growth.
  • S (Synthesis): DNA Replication!
  • DNA content DOUBLES ($2C \rightarrow 4C$).
  • Chromosome number remains the SAME ($2n \rightarrow 2n$).
  • $G_2$ $\rightarrow$ Preparation for division.

The Split: Anaphase I vs II

Anaphase I

Homologous Chromosomes separate.

Centromeres DO NOT split.

Reduces ploidy ($2n \rightarrow n$).

Anaphase II (and Mitosis)

Sister Chromatids separate.

Centromeres SPLIT.

Maintains ploidy.

Key Takeaways

  • S Phase: DNA doubles (4C), Chromosomes stay same (2n).
  • Crossing over occurs in Pachytene.
  • Chiasmata are clearly visible in Diplotene.
  • Mitosis = Equational. Meiosis = Reductional.
