Mendel's Three Laws

  • Law of Dominance: One allele masks the other (Tall over dwarf).
  • Law of Segregation: Alleles separate during gamete formation. Universally true!
  • Law of Independent Assortment: Traits sort independently (if genes are on different chromosomes).

Monohybrid Cross ($Tt \times Tt$)

Phenotypic Ratio = 3:1 | Genotypic Ratio = 1:2:1

Exceptions to Dominance

Incomplete Dominance

Heterozygote is a blend.

Red x White = Pink.

Ratio: 1:2:1 (Both Pheno & Geno)

Co-dominance

Both alleles expressed equally.

Blood Group AB ($I^A I^B$).

Ratio: 1:2:1

The Test Cross

Unknown dominant $\times$ Homozygous recessive

1:1

Dihybrid Test Cross = 1:1:1:1

Key Takeaways

  • Law of Segregation has NO exceptions.
  • Dihybrid phenotypic ratio: 9:3:3:1.
  • Incomplete dominance ratio (Pink flowers) = 1:2:1.
  • ABO Blood group = Co-dominance & Multiple alleles.
