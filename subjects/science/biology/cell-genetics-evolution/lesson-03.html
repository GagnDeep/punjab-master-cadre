Molecular Basis of Inheritance

ਵਿਰਾਸਤ ਦਾ ਅਣੂ ਆਧਾਰ (Molecular Basis)

Why this matters (ERB Standard Pattern)

ERB loves the Central Dogma. Questions testing your knowledge of transcription direction ($5' \rightarrow 3'$ vs $3' \rightarrow 5'$), the specific roles of RNA polymerases, and calculating nucleotide percentages using Chargaff's rules are almost guaranteed.

ਪੇਪਰ ਵਿੱਚ Central Dogma ਅਤੇ Chargaff's rules ਬਾਰੇ ਸਵਾਲ ਪੱਕੇ ਆਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ। ਖਾਸ ਕਰਕੇ $5' \rightarrow 3'$ ਵਾਲੀਆਂ ਦਿਸ਼ਾਵਾਂ ਬਾਰੇ ਧਿਆਨ ਰੱਖਣਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ ਹੈ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Understand the structure of DNA and apply Chargaff's Rules.
  • Describe the Central Dogma: Replication $\rightarrow$ Transcription $\rightarrow$ Translation.
  • Identify the roles of mRNA, tRNA, and rRNA.

1. DNA Structure & Chargaff's Rules

DNA is a double helix made of nucleotides. Each nucleotide has a phosphate group, a deoxyribose sugar, and a nitrogenous base (A, T, G, C).

Chargaff's Rule: In a double-stranded DNA molecule, the amount of Adenine equals Thymine ($A = T$), and the amount of Guanine equals Cytosine ($G = C$).

$$ \frac{A + G}{T + C} = 1 $$

Note: The ratio of $(A+T)/(G+C)$ is constant for a given species, but it is NOT 1.

2. The Central Dogma

The flow of genetic information in a cell goes in one direction:

DNA $\xrightarrow{\text{Transcription}}$ mRNA $\xrightarrow{\text{Translation}}$ Protein

Transcription (DNA to RNA)

  • Enzyme: RNA Polymerase.
  • Direction: Polymerization always happens in the $5' \rightarrow 3'$ direction.
  • The template strand of DNA is read in the $3' \rightarrow 5'$ direction.
  • Uracil (U) replaces Thymine (T) in RNA.

Translation (RNA to Protein)

  • mRNA (messenger RNA) carries the code (codons).
  • tRNA (transfer RNA) brings amino acids to the ribosome. It has an anticodon that pairs with the mRNA codon.
  • rRNA (ribosomal RNA) makes up the ribosome and acts as a catalyst (ribozyme).

Worked Example: Chargaff's Math

Q: If a double-stranded DNA has 20% Cytosine, what is the percentage of Adenine?

Step 1: Apply Chargaff's rule. If $C = 20\%$, then $G = 20\%$.

Step 2: Calculate the total percentage of G + C. $20 + 20 = 40\%$.

Step 3: The remaining 60% must be made of A + T.

Step 4: Since $A = T$, Adenine must be half of the remaining 60%.

Answer: Adenine = $60 / 2 = 30\%$.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

They often give you a DNA sequence in the $3' \rightarrow 5'$ direction and ask for the transcribed mRNA.
For example: If Template DNA is $3'-TACGTA-5'$.
The mRNA will be built $5' \rightarrow 3'$.
T pairs with A, A pairs with U, C pairs with G, etc.
Answer: mRNA is $5'-AUGCAU-3'$.

Quick Recap

  • Chargaff's Rule: $A=T$ (2 H-bonds) and $G=C$ (3 H-bonds).
  • DNA $\rightarrow$ RNA = Transcription (Nucleus).
  • RNA $\rightarrow$ Protein = Translation (Cytoplasm/Ribosome).
  • All polymerases build new strands in the $5' \rightarrow 3'$ direction.
