Newton's Laws & Constraints

Physics Module: Classical Mechanics

Frames of Reference

  • Inertial Frame: At rest or constant velocity ($a = 0$). Newton's laws apply directly.
  • Non-Inertial Frame: Accelerating ($a \neq 0$). Newton's laws FAIL without adjustment.
  • The Fix: Introduce a Pseudo-force.

The Pseudo-Force

$ \vec{F}_p = -m\vec{a}_0 $

Always applied opposite to the frame's acceleration.

FBD: Elevator Accelerating Upwards

Virtual Work Method (Pulleys)

Net work done by internal tension is zero:

$ \sum \vec{T} \cdot \vec{a} = 0 $
  • If $T$ pulls mass $A$ with acceleration $a_A$...
  • And $2T$ pulls mass $B$ with acceleration $a_B$...
  • Then $T(a_A) + 2T(-a_B) = 0 \implies a_A = 2a_B$.

Key Takeaways

  • Non-inertial frame = requires Pseudo-force.
  • Pseudo-force = $-m\vec{a}_0$ (Opposite to frame).
  • Apparent weight (Upward accel) = $m(g+a)$.
  • Apparent weight (Downward accel) = $m(g-a)$.
  • Free fall = Weightlessness ($T=0$).
