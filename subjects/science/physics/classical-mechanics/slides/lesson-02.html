Conservative Forces & Potential Energy

  • Work done is independent of the path.
  • Work done in a closed loop is ZERO.
  • We can define a Potential Energy function $U(x)$.

Force from Potential Energy

$ F = -\frac{dU}{dx} $

Don't forget the negative sign! Force pushes towards lower potential energy.

Types of Collisions

Elastic ($e = 1$)

  • Momentum Conserved
  • Kinetic Energy Conserved
  • Billiard balls (approx)

Perfectly Inelastic ($e = 0$)

  • Momentum Conserved
  • Max Kinetic Energy LOST
  • Objects stick together

Key Takeaways

  • $F = -dU/dx$
  • Momentum is ALWAYS conserved in collisions (if $F_{ext}=0$).
  • Shortcut for Perfectly Inelastic KE Loss: Fraction = $\frac{M}{m+M}$
