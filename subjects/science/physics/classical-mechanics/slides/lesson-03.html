Standard Moments of Inertia ($I$)

Acceleration on Incline

$ a = \frac{g \sin\theta}{1 + \frac{k^2}{R^2}} $

Smaller $\frac{k^2}{R^2}$ means faster acceleration!

The "Race" Down the Incline

Mass and Radius do not matter.

  • 1st: Solid Sphere ($\frac{k^2}{R^2} = 0.4$)
  • 2nd: Solid Cylinder ($\frac{k^2}{R^2} = 0.5$)
  • 3rd: Hollow Sphere ($\frac{k^2}{R^2} \approx 0.67$)
  • 4th: Hollow Cylinder / Ring ($\frac{k^2}{R^2} = 1$)

Key Takeaways

  • $I_{ring} = MR^2$
  • $I_{disc} = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$
  • Solid things roll faster than hollow things.
  • Pure rolling: $v = \omega R$ (No slipping).
