Newton's Laws and Constraints

ਨਿਊਟਨ ਦੇ ਨਿਯਮ ਅਤੇ ਪਾਬੰਦੀਆਂ (Constraints)

Why this matters (ERB 2021 & 2022)

Questions involving pulleys and wedge constraints appear consistently. In 2022, a direct application of pseudo-force in an accelerating elevator was asked. You must master free-body diagrams (FBDs) to solve these under 60 seconds.

ਪੁਲੀ (pulley) ਅਤੇ ਵੇਜ (wedge) ਦੀਆਂ ਸਮੱਸਿਆਵਾਂ ਹਰ ਸਾਲ ਆਉਂਦੀਆਂ ਹਨ। ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ 60 ਸਕਿੰਟਾਂ ਦੇ ਅੰਦਰ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਨੂੰ ਹੱਲ ਕਰਨ ਲਈ ਫ੍ਰੀ-ਬਾਡੀ ਡਾਇਗ੍ਰਾਮਸ (FBDs) ਵਿੱਚ ਮੁਹਾਰਤ ਹਾਸਲ ਕਰਨੀ ਪਵੇਗੀ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Identify inertial and non-inertial frames of reference.
  • Correctly apply pseudo-forces in accelerating frames.
  • Write and solve constraint equations for string-pulley systems quickly.

1. Inertial vs Non-Inertial Frames

An inertial frame is either at rest or moving with a constant velocity. Newton's laws are directly applicable here. A non-inertial frame is accelerating. To apply Newton's laws in a non-inertial frame, we must introduce a fictional force called a pseudo-force.

The pseudo-force \( \vec{F}_p \) on a mass \( m \) in a frame accelerating with \( \vec{a}_0 \) is:

$$ \vec{F}_p = -m\vec{a}_0 $$
Common Trap

Students often apply the pseudo-force in the same direction as the frame's acceleration. Always remember the negative sign: it acts opposite to the frame's acceleration.

2. Constraint Equations (String and Pulley)

When bodies are connected by an inextensible string, their motions are constrained. The length of the string \( L \) remains constant.

Trick for Virtual Work Method: The net work done by internal tension forces on the system is zero. $$ \sum \vec{T} \cdot \vec{v} = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \sum \vec{T} \cdot \vec{a} = 0 $$

If tension \( T \) pulls mass \( A \) with acceleration \( a_A \) and \( 2T \) pulls mass \( B \) with acceleration \( a_B \), then \( T a_A + 2T (-a_B) = 0 \implies a_A = 2a_B \).

Worked Example: Accelerating Elevator

Q: A mass of 5 kg is hanging from a spring balance in an elevator accelerating upwards at \( 2 \, \text{m/s}^2 \). What is the reading of the balance? (Take \( g = 10 \, \text{m/s}^2 \))

Step 1: Frame is non-inertial (accelerating up at \( a_0 = 2 \)).

Step 2: Draw FBD with respect to elevator. Forces acting on mass: Tension \( T \) (up), Gravity \( mg \) (down), Pseudo-force \( ma_0 \) (down, opposite to elevator).

Step 3: Since mass is at rest relative to the elevator: $$ T = mg + ma_0 = m(g + a_0) $$

Answer: \( T = 5(10 + 2) = 60 \, \text{N} \). Reading = 6 kg-wt.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

The examiner will ask for the apparent weight in an elevator accelerating downwards with \( a = g \). In this free-fall case, \( T = m(g - g) = 0 \). They often hide the "downward acceleration" in a complex word problem. Always isolate the direction of acceleration first.

Quick Recap

  • Pseudo-force is \( -m\vec{a}_0 \), applied opposite to frame acceleration.
  • For inextensible strings, \( \sum \vec{T} \cdot \vec{a} = 0 \).
  • Apparent weight in elevator moving up is \( m(g+a) \), and moving down is \( m(g-a) \).

ਸਭ ਤੋਂ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ, ਸੂਡੋ-ਫੋਰਸ (Pseudo-force) ਕੀ ਹੁੰਦੀ ਹੈ? ਸੋਚੋ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਇੱਕ ਤੇਜ਼ ਚੱਲਦੀ ਬੱਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੜ੍ਹੇ ਹੋ, ਜਦੋਂ ਬੱਸ ਅਚਾਨਕ ਬ੍ਰੇਕ ਮਾਰਦੀ ਹੈ ਤਾਂ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਅੱਗੇ ਵੱਲ ਡਿੱਗਦੇ ਹੋ। ਇਹ ਕਿਉਂ ਹੁੰਦਾ ਹੈ? ਕਿਉਂਕਿ ਬੱਸ ਇੱਕ non-inertial frame ਬਣ ਗਈ ਹੈ। ਜਦੋਂ ਵੀ frame ਪ੍ਰਵੇਗ (accelerate) ਕਰਦਾ ਹੈ, ਤਾਂ ਸਾਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਉਲਟ ਦਿਸ਼ਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਫੋਰਸ ਲਗਾਉਣੀ ਪੈਂਦੀ ਹੈ, ਜਿਸਨੂੰ pseudo-force ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਹਨ। ਇਸਦਾ ਫਾਰਮੂਲਾ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੌਖਾ ਹੈ: \( -ma \)। ਯਾਦ ਰੱਖਿਓ, ਮਾਈਨਸ ਦਾ ਮਤਲਬ ਹੈ ਦਿਸ਼ਾ ਉਲਟ ਹੋਣੀ ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ।
