Rigid Body Dynamics

ਸਖ਼ਤ ਸਰੀਰ ਗਤੀਸ਼ੀਲਤਾ (Rigid Body Dynamics)

Why this matters (ERB Favorite)

Questions comparing the acceleration of rings, solid cylinders, and spheres rolling down an incline are guaranteed. If you don't know the standard moments of inertia, you will lose easy marks.

ਢਲਾਨ (incline) 'ਤੇ ਰੋਲ ਕਰਦੀਆਂ ਵਸਤੂਆਂ ਦੇ ਪ੍ਰਵੇਗ (acceleration) ਦੀ ਤੁਲਨਾ ਵਾਲੇ ਸਵਾਲ ਪੱਕੇ ਆਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ। ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ ਇਹਨਾਂ ਦੇ moment of inertia ਜ਼ੁਬਾਨੀ ਯਾਦ ਹੋਣੇ ਚਾਹੀਦੇ ਹਨ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Memorize the Moment of Inertia for standard shapes.
  • Apply the Parallel and Perpendicular Axis Theorems.
  • Calculate acceleration and velocity for objects in pure rolling on an incline.

1. Moment of Inertia (I)

The rotational equivalent of mass. It depends not just on mass, but how it is distributed around the axis.

  • Ring: \( I = MR^2 \)
  • Solid Cylinder / Disc: \( I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2 \)
  • Hollow Sphere: \( I = \frac{2}{3}MR^2 \)
  • Solid Sphere: \( I = \frac{2}{5}MR^2 \)

The factor multiplying \( MR^2 \) is often called \( k^2/R^2 \), where \( k \) is the radius of gyration. Smaller \( k^2/R^2 \) means the mass is closer to the center, making it easier to rotate.

2. Pure Rolling on an Incline

In pure rolling (\( v = \omega R \)), friction does no work, so mechanical energy is conserved.

The acceleration of an object rolling down an incline of angle \( \theta \) is:

$$ a = \frac{g \sin\theta}{1 + \frac{k^2}{R^2}} $$

Notice the denominator. An object with a smaller \( I \) (smaller \( k^2/R^2 \)) will have a larger acceleration and reach the bottom first.

Worked Example: Racing Shapes

Q: A solid sphere and a solid cylinder of the same mass and radius are released from rest at the top of an inclined plane. Which reaches the bottom first?

Step 1: Identify the \( k^2/R^2 \) values.

Solid Sphere: \( \frac{2}{5} = 0.4 \)

Solid Cylinder: \( \frac{1}{2} = 0.5 \)

Step 2: Compare accelerations. \( a \propto \frac{1}{1 + k^2/R^2} \). The sphere has a smaller denominator, so it has a larger acceleration.

Answer: The solid sphere reaches the bottom first.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

ERB will try to trick you by saying "A sphere of mass 10kg and a cylinder of mass 2kg". Mass and radius DO NOT MATTER in the race. Acceleration only depends on the shape factor (\( k^2/R^2 \)).

Quick Recap

  • Ring (\( 1 \)) > Hollow Sphere (\( 2/3 \)) > Solid Cylinder (\( 1/2 \)) > Solid Sphere (\( 2/5 \)).
  • Smaller \( I \) = Faster acceleration down the incline.
  • Pure rolling means \( v_{cm} = \omega R \).
