Chemistry Module: Atomic Structure & Bonding

The Steric Number Shortcut

$ SN = \frac{1}{2}(V + M - C + A) $

V: Valence $e^-$ on Central Atom
M: Monovalent atoms (H, F, Cl, Br, I). Ignore O!
C: Cation Charge (-)
A: Anion Charge (+)

SN $\rightarrow$ Hybridization $\rightarrow$ Geometry

  • SN = 2: $sp$ (Linear)
  • SN = 3: $sp^2$ (Trigonal Planar)
  • SN = 4: $sp^3$ (Tetrahedral)
  • SN = 5: $sp^3d$ (Trigonal Bipyramidal)
  • SN = 6: $sp^3d^2$ (Octahedral)
  • LP = SN - Surrounding Atoms

Shape of $H_2O$ (Water)

The Classic Trick: $I_3^-$

  • Think of it as $I$ attached to two other $I$'s.
  • Central I: $V=7$. Surrounding: $M=2$. Anion: $A=1$.
  • $SN = \frac{1}{2}(7 + 2 - 0 + 1) = 5$ $\implies sp^3d$.
  • Lone Pairs = $5 - 2 = 3$.
  • Shape: The 3 lone pairs sit on the equator. Shape is strictly Linear!

Key Takeaways

  • Calculate SN to find hybridization ($sp^3, sp^3d$, etc).
  • Ignore Oxygen when counting 'M' for SN.
  • Geometry = Electron arrangement (includes LPs).
  • Shape = What the atoms look like (ignores LPs visually).
  • Repulsion: LP-LP > LP-BP > BP-BP.
