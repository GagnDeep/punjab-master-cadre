Chemistry Module: Atomic Structure & Bonding

The Three Golden Rules

  • Aufbau Principle: Fill lowest energy first (use $n+l$ rule).
  • Pauli Exclusion: Max 2 electrons per orbital, opposite spins.
  • Hund's Rule: For degenerate orbitals (same energy), fill singly first before pairing.

Hund's Rule: $p^3$ configuration

The Exceptions: Extra Stability

Chromium (Z=24)

Expected: $4s^2 \, 3d^4$

Actual: $4s^1 \, 3d^5$

(Half-filled stability)

Copper (Z=29)

Expected: $4s^2 \, 3d^9$

Actual: $4s^1 \, 3d^{10}$

(Fully-filled stability)

Forming Cations

Remove from highest $n$ first!

For transition metals: Remove 4s electrons BEFORE 3d electrons.

Key Takeaways

  • $n+l$ rule determines orbital energy order.
  • Hund's rule: Singly occupy before pairing.
  • Symmetrical $d^5$ and $d^{10}$ configurations are extra stable (Exchange energy).
  • Cu is $[Ar] 4s^1 3d^{10}$ and Cr is $[Ar] 4s^1 3d^5$.
