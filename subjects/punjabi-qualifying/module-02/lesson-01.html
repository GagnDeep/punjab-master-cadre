1. Foundational Concepts

Punjabi is written in the Gurmukhi script. The word 'Gurmukhi' translates to 'from the mouth of the Guru'. The alphabet originally consisted of 35 letters, earning it the name 'Painti Akhri'. However, to accommodate sounds from Persian and other languages, six more letters were added by placing a dot (bindi) at the foot of existing letters (ਸ਼, ਖ਼, ਗ਼, ਜ਼, ਫ਼, ਲ਼), bringing the total to 41. 2. Advanced Application

In Gurmukhi, there are three primary vowel bearers (Swar Uchar): Oora (ੳ), Aira (ਅ), and Eeri (ੲ). These three letters cannot be used independently as consonants; instead, they serve as the base for attaching the 10 vowel symbols (lagaan).
