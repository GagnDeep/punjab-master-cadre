Module E: Folk Literature & Culture

Coming Soon

ਮੋਡਿਊਲ ਈ: ਲੋਕ ਸਾਹਿਤ ਅਤੇ ਸੱਭਿਆਚਾਰ

This module dives into Lok Sahit (Folk Literature) and Punjabi Sabhiachar (Culture).

Lessons Outline

  • Lesson 1: Forms of Lok Sahit

    Boli, Tappa, Suhag, Ghorian, Sithnian.

  • Lesson 2: Folk Tales and Lore

    Pari-kahanian, Neeti-kahanian, Dant-kathawan (legends).

  • Lesson 3: Punjabi Sabhiachar

    Rites of passage, festivals, folk dances (Bhangra, Giddha), and traditional dress.
