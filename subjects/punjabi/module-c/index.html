Module C: Fiction & Drama

Coming Soon

ਮੋਡਿਊਲ ਸੀ: ਗਲਪ ਅਤੇ ਨਾਟਕ

This module covers Kahani (Story), Novel, Natak (Drama), and Ikangi (One-act play).

Lessons Outline

  • Lesson 1: Origin of Punjabi Novel

    Bhai Vir Singh and early novelistic forms.

  • Lesson 2: Nanak Singh

    The father of the Punjabi novel, Chitta Lahu, Pavitar Papi.

  • Lesson 3: Gurdial Singh & Modern Novelists

    Marhi Da Deeva, regionalism, and psychological novels.

  • Lesson 4: Punjabi Kahani (Short Story)

    Sujan Singh, Sant Singh Sekhon, Kulwant Singh Virk.

  • Lesson 5: Contemporary Short Stories

    Ajit Kaur, Prem Prakash, and modern themes.

  • Lesson 6: Origin of Punjabi Drama (Natak)

    I.C. Nanda and the birth of the stage play.

  • Lesson 7: Major Dramatists

    Balwant Gargi, Harcharan Singh, Kapoor Singh Ghuman.

  • Lesson 8: Ikangi (One-Act Play)

    Structure, history, and prominent writers of Ikangi.
