Bhai Vir Singh & Amrita Pritam

ਭਾਈ ਵੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਅਤੇ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਾ ਪ੍ਰੀਤਮ

Why this matters (ERB Pattern)

Bhai Vir Singh is the father of modern Punjabi poetry, and Amrita Pritam is its most famous female voice. ERB often asks about their specific anthologies and Sahitya Akademi awards.

ਆਧੁਨਿਕ ਕਵਿਤਾ ਵਿੱਚ ਇਹ ਦੋਵੇਂ ਨਾਮ ਸਭ ਤੋਂ ਮਹੱਤਵਪੂਰਨ ਹਨ।

By the end of this lesson, you will:

  • Identify Bhai Vir Singh as the pioneer of modern Punjabi poetry.
  • Recognize Amrita Pritam's contribution and her iconic poem 'Ajj Aakhaan Waris Shah Nu'.
  • Memorize their major award-winning works.

1. Bhai Vir Singh

Considered the father of modern Punjabi literature. He introduced short poems and blank verse to Punjabi.

His poetry focuses on nature, mysticism, and Sikh philosophy. Major work: 'Mere Saiyan Jio' (Sahitya Akademi Award).

2. Amrita Pritam

A leading romantic and progressive voice. Her poignant poem addressing Waris Shah during the 1947 partition is universally acclaimed.

She won the Sahitya Akademi Award for 'Sunehade' and the Jnanpith Award for 'Kagaz Te Canvas'.

Worked Example

Q: For which work did Amrita Pritam receive the Jnanpith Award?

Step 1: Recall Amrita Pritam's major awards.

Step 2: Differentiate between Sahitya Akademi and Jnanpith.

Answer: Kagaz Te Canvas.

🧠 How ERB Asks It

ERB loves award questions. Always match the author, the book, and the award correctly.

Quick Recap

  • Bhai Vir Singh: Father of modern Punjabi poetry.
  • Amrita Pritam: Voice of the partition pain.
  • Memorize 'Mere Saiyan Jio' and 'Kagaz Te Canvas'.
