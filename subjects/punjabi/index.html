Complete Course for 150 MCQs / 150 Minutes Exam

150 Marks No Negative Marking 36 Total Lessons

Study Plan (Phase Guidance)

Phase 1: Foundation

Modules A & B. Master the vast landscape of Punjabi poetry and major prose writers. These carry the most weight and require deep context.

Phase 2: Core Areas

Modules C, D & E. Cover fiction, drama, autobiographies, and Punjabi folk culture. Focus on specific literary forms and cultural terms.

Phase 3: Revision & Rules

Modules F & G. Solidify grammar (Vyakaran) rules and pedagogy concepts. Test your speed with timed quizzes.
