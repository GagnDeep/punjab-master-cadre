Module F: Grammar (Vyakaran)

Coming Soon

ਮੋਡਿਊਲ ਐਫ: ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਵਿਆਕਰਨ

Complete coverage of Punjabi linguistics, grammar rules, and structural analysis.

Lessons Outline

  • Lesson 1: Bhasha and Gurmukhi Lipi

    Origin of Punjabi language, dialects (Majhi, Malwai, Doabi, Puadhi), and script history.

  • Lesson 2: Dhuni Bodh (Phonology)

    Swar (Vowels), Vianjan (Consonants), Laga-matra, and Lgakhar.

  • Lesson 3: Shabad Bodh - 1 (Morphology)

    Naav (Noun), Parnaav (Pronoun), and Visheshan (Adjective).

  • Lesson 4: Shabad Bodh - 2

    Kirya (Verb), Kirya Visheshan (Adverb), Sabandhak (Preposition), Yojak (Conjunction).

  • Lesson 5: Vaak Bodh (Syntax)

    Sentence structure, simple/compound/complex sentences, and Visram Chinn (Punctuation).

  • Lesson 6: Arath Bodh (Semantics)

    Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms (Muhavare), and Proverbs (Akhaan).
