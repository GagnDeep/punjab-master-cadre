Study Plan & Phase Guidance

Phase 1: Foundation

Start with Punjab History and Indian Polity. These form the core of the exam and carry significant weightage. Build your conceptual clarity.

Phase 2: Core Concepts

Move on to Geography and Micro/Macro Economics. Focus on maps, geographical features, and economic principles with practical examples.

Phase 3: Revision & Speed

Take timed mock tests. Review Pedagogy and Punjab Current Affairs. Since there is no negative marking, practice attempting all 150 questions in 150 minutes.
