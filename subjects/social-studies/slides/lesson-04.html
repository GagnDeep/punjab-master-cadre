Maharaja Ranjit Singh

Consolidation and Administration

ਮਹਾਰਾਜਾ ਰਣਜੀਤ ਸਿੰਘ: ਪ੍ਰਸ਼ਾਸਨ ਅਤੇ ਜਿੱਤਾਂ

Rise to Power

  • Born into the Sukerchakia Misl.
  • Captured Lahore in 1799 from the Bhangi Misl.
  • Crowned Maharaja on Baisakhi day in 1801.
  • Captured Amritsar, the religious capital, in 1805.

Treaty of Amritsar (1809)

The Key Boundary

The treaty with the British established the River Sutlej as the firm boundary between the Sikh Empire and British India.

Timeline of Major Conquests

  • 1799: Lahore
  • 1805: Amritsar
  • 1818: Multan
  • 1819: Kashmir
  • 1834: Peshawar

Pillars of the Empire

Hari Singh Nalwa

The legendary general who secured the Afghan frontier (Peshawar). Martyred at Jamrud (1837).

Secular Cabinet

Prime Minister: Dhian Singh (Dogra)
Foreign Minister: Fakir Azizuddin (Muslim)
Finance: Dina Nath (Hindu)

Military Modernization (Fauj-i-Khas)

Ranjit Singh hired European mercenaries (many from Napoleon's army) to modernize his forces:

  • Jean-Baptiste Ventura: Trained the Infantry (Fauj-i-Khas).
  • Jean-François Allard: Trained the Cavalry.
  • Claude Auguste Court: Modernized the Artillery.

The Capital Captured

1799

The year Ranjit Singh captured Lahore, laying the foundation of the empire.

Summary Recap

  • Treaty of 1809: Sutlej boundary with British.
  • Conquests: Multan (1818), Kashmir (1819).
  • Hari Singh Nalwa: Hero of the NW Frontier.
  • Ventura & Allard: European generals training the army.
