Banda Singh Bahadur & The Sikh Misls

18th Century Punjab: Struggle to Sovereignty

ਬੰਦਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਬਹਾਦਰ ਅਤੇ ਸਿੱਖ ਮਿਸਲਾਂ

Baba Banda Singh Bahadur

  • Original Name: Lachhman Dev (later Madho Das).
  • Met Guru Gobind Singh Ji at Nanded in 1708.
  • Led the Sikh forces to establish the first independent sovereign state.
  • Established his capital at Lohgarh.

Key Achievements

  • Battle of Chappar Chiri (1710): Defeated and killed Wazir Khan (Governor of Sirhind).
  • Minted coins in the name of Guru Nanak and Guru Gobind Singh.
  • Abolished the Zamindari System: Gave land ownership rights to the tillers (farmers).

Formation of Dal Khalsa (1748)

The Organizer

Nawab Kapur Singh united the scattered Sikh jathas on Baisakhi day in 1748 at Amritsar.

The Structure

Divided into two main bodies:

  • Buddha Dal: Veterans (defense & religious duties).
  • Taruna Dal: Youth (combat & offensive ops).

The 12 Sikh Misls

As Mughal authority collapsed, the Dal Khalsa reorganized into 12 sovereign military brotherhoods (Misls). "Misl" means equal.

  • Ahluwalia: Jassa Singh
  • Bhangi: Chhajja Singh (Most powerful initially)
  • Sukerchakia: Charat Singh
  • Ramgarhia: Jassa Singh Ramgarhia
  • Kanhiya: Jai Singh
  • Phulkian: Chaudhari Phul

Battle of Chappar Chiri

1710

The year Banda Singh Bahadur defeated Wazir Khan, avenging the younger Sahibzadas.

Summary Recap

  • Lohgarh: Capital of Banda Singh Bahadur.
  • Zamindari: Abolished by Banda Singh Bahadur.
  • Dal Khalsa (1748): Organized by Nawab Kapur Singh.
  • Sukerchakia Misl: The Misl of Maharaja Ranjit Singh.
