The Sikh Gurus (Part 1)

Guru Nanak Dev Ji to Guru Arjan Dev Ji

ਸਿੱਖ ਗੁਰੂ ਸਾਹਿਬਾਨ (ਪਹਿਲੀ ਤੋਂ ਪੰਜਵੀਂ ਪਾਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ)

1. Guru Nanak Dev Ji (1469-1539)

  • Founder of Sikhism. Born in Rai Bhoi Ki Talwandi.
  • Undertook 4 major journeys (Udasis).
  • Established Sangat (congregation) and Pangat (Langar).
  • Settled in Kartarpur on the river Ravi.

2. Guru Angad Dev Ji (1504-1552)

  • Original name: Bhai Lehna.
  • Standardized the Gurmukhi script.
  • Established the Mall Akhara for physical fitness.
  • Founded the city of Khadur Sahib.

3. Guru Amar Das Ji (1479-1574)

  • "Pehle Pangat, Piche Sangat" - strengthened Langar system.
  • Constructed a Baoli (84 steps) at Goindwal Sahib.
  • Established the Manji System (22 dioceses).

Manji vs. Masand System

Manji System

Started by Guru Amar Das Ji (3rd Guru).

Purpose: Preaching across 22 regions.

Masand System

Started by Guru Ram Das Ji (4th Guru).

Purpose: Collecting funds (Dasvandh) for construction.

4. Guru Ram Das Ji (1534-1581)

  • Original name: Bhai Jetha.
  • Founded Ramdaspur (modern-day Amritsar).
  • Started the excavation of the holy Sarovar.
  • Composed the Lavan (marriage hymns).

5. Guru Arjan Dev Ji (1563-1606)

  • Completed the Amritsar Sarovar.
  • Built Harmandir Sahib (foundation by Mian Mir).
  • Compiled the Adi Granth in 1604.
  • Founded Tarn Taran, Kartarpur (Jalandhar).
  • Became the first Sikh Martyr in 1606.

Compilation Year

1604

The year Guru Arjan Dev Ji compiled the Adi Granth.

  • Khadur Sahib: Guru Angad Dev Ji
  • Goindwal Sahib: Guru Amar Das Ji
  • Amritsar: Guru Ram Das Ji
  • Tarn Taran: Guru Arjan Dev Ji
