Freedom Movement in Punjab

Kukas, Ghadar, and Bhagat Singh

ਪੰਜਾਬ ਵਿੱਚ ਸੁਤੰਤਰਤਾ ਸੰਗਰਾਮ

The Namdhari (Kuka) Movement

  • Founded by Baba Ram Singh at Bhaini Sahib.
  • First to boycott British goods and educational institutions.
  • 1872: 66 Kukas were blown away from cannons at Malerkotla.
  • Baba Ram Singh was exiled to Rangoon.

The Ghadar Party (1913)

Origins

Founded in San Francisco, USA to overthrow British rule via armed rebellion.

Key Figures

  • President: Sohan Singh Bhakna
  • Leader: Lala Hardayal

Komagata Maru Incident (1914)

  • A Japanese steamship chartered by Baba Gurdit Singh.
  • Carried 376 Indians to challenge Canadian immigration laws.
  • Denied entry at Vancouver and returned to India.
  • Passengers were fired upon by British police at Budge Budge (Calcutta).

Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

  • Date: April 13, 1919 (Baisakhi).
  • Protesting the Rowlatt Act and the arrest of Dr. Kitchlew and Dr. Satyapal.
  • Brigadier-General Dyer ordered troops to fire without warning.
  • Resulted in hundreds of deaths and fueled national outrage.

The Tragic Date

April 13, 1919

The date of the Jallianwala Bagh Massacre.

Shaheed Bhagat Singh

  • Founded the Naujawan Bharat Sabha in 1926.
  • Assassinated Saunders (1928) to avenge Lala Lajpat Rai.
  • Threw bombs in the Central Assembly (1929) to "make the deaf hear."
  • Hanged on March 23, 1931 along with Rajguru and Sukhdev.

Timeline Summary

  • 1872: Malerkotla Kuka executions.
  • 1913: Ghadar Party in San Francisco.
  • 1914: Komagata Maru incident.
  • 1919: Jallianwala Bagh.
  • 1931: Martyrdom of Bhagat Singh.
