Guru Hargobind Ji to Guru Gobind Singh Ji

ਸਿੱਖ ਗੁਰੂ ਸਾਹਿਬਾਨ (ਛੇਵੀਂ ਤੋਂ ਦਸਵੀਂ ਪਾਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ)

6. Guru Hargobind Ji (1595-1644)

  • Introduced the concept of Miri (temporal) and Piri (spiritual).
  • Constructed the Akal Takht.
  • Fought four successful defensive battles against Mughal forces.
  • Built the fort of Lohgarh in Amritsar.

7 & 8. Guru Har Rai Ji & Guru Harkrishan Ji

Guru Har Rai Ji

Maintained a large army but avoided conflict. Ran a free Ayurvedic clinic at Kiratpur.

Guru Harkrishan Ji

The "Bala Pritam" (Child Guru). Served victims of smallpox in Delhi. His last words: "Baba Bakale".

9. Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji (1621-1675)

  • Founded the city of Chakk Nanaki (now Anandpur Sahib).
  • Approached by Kashmiri Pandits for protection against forced conversion.
  • Martyred at Chandni Chowk, Delhi in 1675 by Emperor Aurangzeb.
  • Remembered as Hind Di Chadar (Shield of India).

10. Guru Gobind Singh Ji (1666-1708)

  • Born in Patna, Bihar.
  • Created the Khalsa in 1699 at Anandpur Sahib.
  • Introduced the Panj Kakar (Five Ks) and Khande di Pahul (amrit).
  • Endowed the Guruship to the Guru Granth Sahib in 1708.

Birth of the Khalsa

1699

The year Guru Gobind Singh Ji created the Khalsa Panth on Baisakhi.

ਖਾਲਸਾ ਪੰਥ ਦੀ ਸਾਜਨਾ

Major Battles of Guru Gobind Singh Ji

  • Battle of Bhangani (1688): First major battle; defeated hill rajas.
  • Battles of Anandpur: Long sieges against allied Mughal and Hill Raja forces.
  • Battle of Chamkaur (1704): Martyrdom of two elder Sahibzadas.
  • Battle of Muktsar (1705): Sacrifice of the Chali Mukte (40 Liberated Ones).

Key Concepts Recap

  • Miri-Piri: Guru Hargobind Ji
  • Bala Pritam: Guru Harkrishan Ji
  • Hind Di Chadar: Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji
  • Khalsa Creation: Guru Gobind Singh Ji (1699)
