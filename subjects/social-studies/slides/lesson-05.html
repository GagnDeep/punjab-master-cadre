Anglo-Sikh Wars & Annexation

The Fall of the Sikh Empire (1845 - 1849)

ਐਂਗਲੋ-ਸਿੱਖ ਯੁੱਧ ਅਤੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਨੂੰ ਮਿਲਾਉਣਾ

The Chaos (1839 - 1845)

  • Succession crisis after Maharaja Ranjit Singh's death.
  • Young Duleep Singh placed on the throne with Maharani Jindan as regent.
  • Treachery by Dogras (Gulab Singh) and Commanders (Tej Singh, Lal Singh).
  • Khalsa army crossed the Sutlej, sparking the First War.

First Anglo-Sikh War (1845-46)

Key Battles:

  • Mudki
  • Ferozeshah
  • Aliwal
  • Sobraon (1846): The decisive defeat due to betrayal. Martyrdom of Sham Singh Attariwala.

The Treaties of 1846

Treaty of Lahore (March)

Sikhs lost Jalandhar Doab. Kashmir was sold to Gulab Singh for 75 Lakhs to pay war indemnity.

Treaty of Bhairowal (Dec)

Maharani Jindan removed from power. A British Resident (Henry Lawrence) appointed to rule via Regency Council.

Second Anglo-Sikh War (1848-49)

  • Started with Mulraj's rebellion in Multan.
  • Battle of Chillianwala: Fierce Sikh resistance; heavy British casualties.
  • Battle of Gujrat (1849): Known as the "Battle of Guns." Decisive British victory.

The Annexation

March 29, 1849

Lord Dalhousie formally annexed Punjab into the British Empire.

Summary Recap

  • Sobraon (1846): End of First War.
  • Bhairowal Treaty: British Resident installed.
  • Gujrat (1849): Battle of Guns.
  • Lord Dalhousie: Annexed Punjab.
