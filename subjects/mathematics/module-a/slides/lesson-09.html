Common Error

Applying the linear arrangement formula (n!) to a circular table problem. Always use (n-1)! for circles unless the positions are numbered or distinguishable.

How to Avoid

Always double-check your initial assumptions before calculating.

Worked Example

Q: In how many ways can 5 boys and 4 girls sit in a row such that all girls sit together?

Steps:

  • Treat the 4 girls as a single unit.
  • Total units to arrange = 5 boys + 1 (girls unit) = 6 units.
  • Arrange these 6 units: 6! = 720 ways.
  • The 4 girls can be arranged among themselves inside their unit in 4! = 24 ways.
  • Total arrangements = 720 × 24 = 17,280.

Solution

17,280 ways.

Exam Pattern

Derangement problems (where no item occupies its original position) sometimes appear as hard questions. The formula for deranging n objects is D_n = n! * (1 - 1/1! + 1/2! - 1/3! ... + (-1)^n/n!). Memorize D_3 = 2, D_4 = 9, D_5 = 44.

PYQ Focus

Arrangement problems with specific restrictions (like vowels always together) and circular arrangements are extremely common. Understanding the difference between choosing (nCr) and arranging (nPr) is fundamental.

Quick Review

  • Permutation (Order matters): nPr = n! / (n-r)!
  • Combination (Order does not matter): nCr = n! / [r!(n-r)!]
  • Circular arrangements: (n-1)!
  • Repeated items: Divide by factorial of repetitions.
  • Grouping restriction: Treat as one unit, then multiply by internal arrangements.
