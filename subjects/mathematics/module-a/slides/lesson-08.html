Module A • Lesson 08

Quadratic Equations

Objectives

  • Determine the nature of roots using the discriminant (D = b2 - 4ac).
  • Calculate the sum and product of roots rapidly.
  • Form a quadratic equation when its roots are given.
  • Analyze the signs of roots based on the coefficients.
  • Find the maximum or minimum value of a quadratic expression.

Core Concept

For a quadratic equation ax2 + bx + c = 0, the roots are given by the quadratic formula. The expression under the square root, D = b2 - 4ac, is the discriminant. If D > 0, the roots are real and distinct. If D = 0, the roots are real and equal (coincident). If D < 0, the roots are complex conjugates. A profoundly useful shortcut involves the relationship between the roots (α, β) and the coefficients. The sum of the roots is α + β = -ba, and the product is αβ = ca. You can build any quadratic equation if you know the sum (S) and product (P) of its roots: x2 - Sx + P = 0.

Common Error

If the coefficients a, b, c are rational, and one root is an irrational surd (e.g., p + √q), the other root MUST be its conjugate (p - √q). The same rule applies to complex roots only if coefficients are real. Do not assume conjugate roots if coefficients themselves contain imaginary numbers.

How to Avoid

Always double-check your initial assumptions before calculating.

Worked Example

Q: If α and β are roots of x2 - 5x + 6 = 0, find the value of α2 + β2.

Steps:

  • Identify the sum and product from the equation: α + β = 5, and αβ = 6.
  • Use the algebraic identity: α2 + β2 = (α + β)2 - 2αβ.
  • Substitute the known values: (5)2 - 2(6).
  • Calculate the final result: 25 - 12 = 13.

Solution

The value is 13.

Exam Pattern

Questions asking for the minimum or maximum value of a quadratic expression ax2 + bx + c are common. The vertex occurs at x = -b/(2a). If 'a' is positive, it's a minimum; if 'a' is negative, it's a maximum. The actual min/max value is -D/(4a).

PYQ Focus

Determining the nature of roots using the discriminant and applying the sum and product of roots formulas are guaranteed to appear. These are high-yield questions.

Quick Review

  • D = b2 - 4ac determines root nature.
  • Sum of roots = -b/a.
  • Product of roots = c/a.
  • Equation formation: x2 - (Sum)x + (Product) = 0.
  • Vertex (min/max point) is at x = -b/(2a).
