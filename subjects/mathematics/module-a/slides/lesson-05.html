Common Error

Applying the infinite GP sum formula when |r| ≥ 1. The series diverges, and the formula yields a nonsensical, incorrect result. Always verify the ratio first.

How to Avoid

Always double-check your initial assumptions before calculating.

Worked Example

Q: Find the sum of the infinite geometric series: 2, 2/3, 2/9, 2/27, ...

Steps:

  • Identify the first term 'a': a = 2.
  • Calculate the common ratio 'r' by dividing the second term by the first: (2/3) / 2 = 1/3.
  • Verify that |r| < 1. Since 1/3 < 1, the series converges.
  • Apply the infinite sum formula: S = a / (1 - r).
  • Substitute values: S = 2 / (1 - 1/3) = 2 / (2/3) = 3.

Solution

The sum is 3.

Exam Pattern

ERB frequently tests the relationship between the Arithmetic Mean (AM), Geometric Mean (GM), and Harmonic Mean (HM). Memorize the inequality: AM ≥ GM ≥ HM, and the equation GM2 = AM × HM.

PYQ Focus

Questions on finding the sum of infinite geometric series and identifying the nth term of a harmonic progression are staple MCQs. Speed here is crucial.

Quick Review

  • AP nth term: a + (n-1)d
  • GP sum (infinite): a / (1-r) for |r| < 1
  • HP is the reciprocal of AP
  • AM ≥ GM ≥ HM
  • GM2 = AM × HM
