Common Error

A common mistake is incorrectly calculating the argument (angle) in different quadrants. While tan-1(|b/a|) gives the reference angle, you must adjust it based on whether the number is in the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th quadrant.

How to Avoid

Always double-check your initial assumptions before calculating.

Worked Example

Q: Express the complex number (1 + i) / (1 - i) in the standard a + ib form.

Steps:

  • Identify the denominator: 1 - i. Its conjugate is 1 + i.
  • Multiply numerator and denominator by (1 + i).
  • Numerator becomes: (1 + i)(1 + i) = 1 + 2i + i2 = 1 + 2i - 1 = 2i.
  • Denominator becomes: (1 - i)(1 + i) = 12 - i2 = 1 - (-1) = 2.
  • Divide: 2i / 2 = i.

Solution

The standard form is 0 + 1i, or simply i.

Exam Pattern

The cube roots of unity (1, ω, ω2) frequently appear in algebraic simplification questions. Always use the properties: 1 + ω + ω2 = 0 and ω3 = 1 to reduce high powers.

PYQ Focus

Questions on finding the modulus, conjugate, or expressing a fraction in standard a+ib form appear in almost every paper. Polar form conversions are also very common.

Quick Review

  • Powers of i cycle in sequences of 4.
  • Modulus |z| = √(a2 + b2).
  • z × z̄ = |z|2.
  • Argument must be adjusted based on the quadrant.
  • Cube roots of unity: 1 + ω + ω2 = 0.
