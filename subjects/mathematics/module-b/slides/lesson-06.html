Module B • Lesson 06

Differential Equations

Objectives

  • Determine the order and degree of a D.E.
  • Form a D.E. from a family of curves.
  • Solve using Variable Separable method.
  • Find Integrating Factors and solve Linear D.E.s.

Order & Degree

Order: The highest derivative present in the equation.

Degree: The power of that highest derivative.

Caution on Degree

If a derivative is "trapped" inside a function like sin(dy/dx) or edy/dx, the degree is Not Defined.

Forming a D.E.

y = A sin(x) + B cos(x)

How many arbitrary constants? Two (A and B).

Therefore, you must differentiate twice to eliminate them.

Variable Separable

Group all 'y' terms with 'dy' and all 'x' terms with 'dx'.

Then simply integrate both sides.

∫f(y)dy = ∫g(x)dx

Linear First Order D.E.

Standard Form: dy/dx + P(x)y = Q(x)

  • Step 1: Make sure dy/dx has a coefficient of 1.
  • Step 2: Identify P(x) and Q(x).
  • Step 3: Calculate Integrating Factor (I.F.) = e∫P(x)dx.
  • Step 4: Solution is y * (I.F.) = ∫ Q(x) * (I.F.) dx + C.

Worked Example

Q: Find the I.F. for x(dy/dx) + 2y = x3

  • Divide by x: dy/dx + (2/x)y = x2
  • Identify P(x): P(x) = 2/x
  • Integrate P: ∫(2/x)dx = 2 ln(x) = ln(x2)
  • I.F. = eln(x2)

Solution

I.F. = x2

Exam Strategy

Don't jump straight into long calculations. Check if the question only asks for the Order or the I.F.

PYQ Insight

Order and Degree questions are guaranteed. Memorize the rule about "Not Defined" degree to score a quick +1.

Quick Review

  • Order = Highest deriv. Degree = Power of highest deriv.
  • No. of constants = Order of the D.E.
  • Linear Form: dy/dx + P(x)y = Q(x).
  • I.F. = e∫P dx.
