Definite Integrals & Areas

Objectives

  • Master the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
  • Calculate area under and between curves.
  • Leverage definite integral properties for shortcuts.

Fundamental Theorem

∫ab f(x)dx = F(b) - F(a)

Where F(x) is the antiderivative of f(x).

No '+ C' is needed!

Geometric Meaning

Represents the signed area between the curve and the x-axis.

Area Between Curves

Area = ∫ab (Top Curve - Bottom Curve) dx

  • Always sketch the graph first.
  • Find intersection points to determine the limits 'a' and 'b' if not given.

Odd vs Even

  • Odd Function (f(-x) = -f(x)): Integral = 0.
  • Even Function (f(-x) = f(x)): Integral = 2 * ∫0a f(x)dx.

Worked Example

Q: Area under y = x2 from x = 0 to 3.

  • Integral: ∫03 x2 dx
  • Antiderivative: x3/3
  • Evaluate at 3: 33/3 = 27/3 = 9
  • Evaluate at 0: 0
  • Result: 9 - 0 = 9

Solution

9 sq. units

Common Trap

"Evaluate the integral" vs. "Find the Area"

The Difference

Integrals can be negative. Area is strictly positive. If a curve dips below the x-axis, the area is the absolute value of the integral.

Quick Review

  • Definite Integral: F(b) - F(a).
  • Area: Top minus Bottom.
  • Odd functions from -a to a evaluate to 0.
  • Area must be positive.
