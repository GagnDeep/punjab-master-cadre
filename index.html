Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eligibility for the Punjab Master Cadre 2026 exam?

Candidates must have a B.Ed degree, have cleared PSTET-II, and be between 18 and 37 years of age.

Is the Punjabi qualifying paper mandatory?

Yes, it is a compulsory qualifying paper of 50 marks at the 10th-grade level that every aspirant must pass.

Is there negative marking in the exam?

No, there is no negative marking. Our strategy trains you to confidently attempt all 150 questions.
